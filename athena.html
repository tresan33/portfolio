Athena

Background

a little about Athena

Athena was developed as part of a speculative design project that explored how technology might improve life in the future. Rather than focusing on feasibility, the project emphasized the design process and collaboration. Our goal for Athena was to create an educational companion tailored to individual students, with a focus on areas affected by teacher shortages. Drawing inspiration from Adventure Time's BMO, The Legend of Zelda's Navi, and Amazon Echo Dot, Athena's more than just an instructor, but a companion as well.

my role

I led the visual development of the Athena prototype, illustrating multiple design iterations and crafting interface assets for our Figma wireframe. I collaborated closely with teammates to refine Athena’s personality and appearance, integrating feedback to ensure the final design reflected both functionality and emotional resonance.

impact

This project strengthened my skills in prototyping, visual storytelling, and cross-functional collaboration. I learned how to balance imaginative design with user needs, and how to effectively communicate ideas within a team. Athena deepened my interest in designing playful and functional technology.

Wireframe

individualized learning

Influenced by educational tools such as Canvas, we created a simple Figma wireframe to envision what the Athena companion app might look like. The app was designed to help students track classes and assignments while also allowing them to customize the appearance of their Athena bot. This balance of utility and personalization reflects our goal of making learning both organized and engaging.

my thinking

My primary contribution to the wireframe was the Athena logo. Since the project name was inspired by the Greek goddess Athena, I wanted the logo to evoke imagery of ancient Greece. I incorporated a laurel wreath wrapping around the Athena bot to symbolize wisdom and achievement. In the wireframe, the homepage showcases this logo alongside customization options for the bot, as well as educational features that support student learning.
