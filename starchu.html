Starchu Valley Flyer

Background

a little about Taiko Kai

Taiko Kai is a Registered Student Organization (RSO) at the University of Washington in Seattle. They practice and perform kumidaiko, a performing art featuring a drumming ensemble. Each year they host a themed spring concert, with themes ranging from anime to video games.

In 2025, the theme was "Star-chu Valley", based on the popular video game "Stardew Valley" made by Eric Barone (ConcernedApe).

my role

As part of the Design committee for the 2025 spring concert, I was tasked with designing the flyer we would use to advertise the concert.

Process

capturing that "Stardew" charm

I wanted to create a flyer that was distinctly recognizable and eye catching for those who might see it. Pulling inpsiration from the game's logo and user interface, I created this flyer to attract both fans of Stardew Valley and fans of taiko.
