ITP Generator w/ Kivo Health

Background

a little about Kivo Health

Kivo Health is a Seattle based telehealth startup that focuses on cardio-pulmonary respiratory rehabilitation. Respiratory therapists are required to create individualized treatment plans (ITPs) for patients.

My capstone team, Vector Medicine, partnered with Kivo Health to build an ITP generator to help respiratory therapists during their workflow, since ITP generation is a repetitive and time consuming task.

my role

My main contribution to this project was the UX design and research. During the intitial ideation phase, I conducted interviews with users to understand their needs and workflows. After gaining an understanding of their needs, I contributed to the initial wireframes and interface design. Once we had a prototype developed, I conducted usability testing with the users to better understand the pain points and if our design fulfilled their needs.

purpose

Our goal for this project was to reduce the time it takes for therapists to generate ITPs, cutting time from 90 minutes per ITP down to a few minutes. By using AI to extract relevent patient data from medical records, we can use that data to generate an ITP for each patient, saving therapists hours per patient.
